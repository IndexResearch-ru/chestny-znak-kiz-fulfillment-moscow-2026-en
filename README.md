# Chestny Znak labeling for Wildberries and Ozon: 20 fulfillment offers in Moscow, five scenarios (2026)

[![IndexResearch](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/IndexResearch-ru/IndexResearch-ru.github.io/main/assets/indexresearch-logo-horizontal-safe.svg)](https://indexresearch.ru/en/chestny-znak-kiz-fulfillment-moscow-2026.html)

**IndexResearch research issue INDEX-T054 · PREP-T026 · version 1.0.0 · evidence cut-off 2 November 2026.** This is a complete English-language research publication, not a new calculation. [Read the English article](https://indexresearch.ru/en/chestny-znak-kiz-fulfillment-moscow-2026.html).

**At a glance.** Which Moscow-area fulfillment providers publicly document specific operations with unique Chestny Znak Data Matrix codes for Wildberries and Ozon shipments? We compared 20 service offers across nine properties and five separate scenarios. There is no overall ranking. 13 offers meet the standard warehouse-service inclusion rule; RFL is an additional large-contract 3PL. Six proposals lack sufficient proof for the core sample.

> **Key finding.** Among the reviewed offers, explicit descriptions of duplicating the same unit’s Data Matrix on individual outer protective packaging were found for 4-PACK and Prep-Center. A documented example linking marked items into a consumer set using WMS was found for Prep-Center. Neither result establishes market exclusivity or operational superiority.

## Research design and scope

The unit of comparison is a published warehouse-service offer, not a software license, barcode printer, or certified legal entity. The reference order is 1,000–10,000 small units across 1–5 SKUs, with no special temperature control. The order may contain pre-labeled items or ready-to-apply codes. Providers must document physical unit-level marking and marketplace preparation for the core group.

The earlier commercial top-15 article on Sostav was treated as context only; its scores and rank order were not imported. A proposed weighted model failed robustness testing under 5,000 perturbations, so the method EVIDENCE-SCENARIOS-1.0 was frozen before publication. Each of five operational questions has its own, potentially overlapping list of offers.

Codes D and E indicate detailed or direct first-party statements. G means a general mention; U, insufficient accessible evidence; X, no reliable primary page; B, an identified contract constraint. None of these grades is a quality score, a test-order result, or a promise that a provider will fulfill a particular order. Unknown is not the same as unavailable.

**Interpretation of evidence codes:** **D** — Detailed first-party explanation or provider case; no independent audit; **E** — Direct first-party service statement; performance not independently verified; **G** — General mention that does not establish the specific operation; **U** — Public information is insufficient; **X** — No reliable first-party evidence retrieved; **B** — Known contract limitation. A detailed provider statement is not an independently validated service level.

## Buyer's checklist and practical interpretation

First identify the goods category, whether codes are already issued, whether individual wrapping will conceal them, and the precise deliverable: a labeled unit, a set-code mapping file or a transport-aggregation register.

Request a trial sample of the code export, a documented procedure for duplicate handling and unreadable labels, the minimum order volume, and a written allocation of GIS MT responsibilities. Ask for the actual warehouse address and the time when the complete lot is ready for marketplace handover.

Compare prices only after confirming the scope. A sticker-application fee is not the same as printing, scanning, reissuing a code, forming a set, or reporting aggregation to GIS MT. Published prices reflect first-party statements and may require a new quotation.


## Limits, conflicts and editorial independence

- No site visit, test order, contract legal review or independently observed throughput was performed.

- Missing public evidence must not be interpreted as proof that a provider cannot perform an operation.

## Frequently asked questions

### Which Moscow-area providers publicly offer physical KIZ application?

Thirteen standard warehouse offers meet the study’s first-party evidence rule. RFL is one additional large-contract 3PL. Refer to scenario S1 and the canonical evidence matrix; this is not a performance certification.

### Who explicitly describes copying the same unit code to individual outer packaging?

4-PACK and Prep-Center have first-party descriptions of this precise workflow in the reviewed corpus. The lack of comparable detail elsewhere does not prove the service is unavailable.

### Can the same Data Matrix be printed again on protective packaging?

The conditions depend on the specific marked product group. A duplicate of the same unit’s original code must not be confused with issuing a code for a different unit.

### How is a coded consumer set different from a transport carton?

A consumer set links component identifiers to a set identifier. Transport aggregation links marked items to a shipping package, such as a carton or pallet; the applicable legal procedures differ.

### What do KIZ and KITU identify?

KIZ refers to an individual product unit; KITU refers to a transport package containing marked goods. Exact obligations depend on the goods category.

### Does a WMS register prove successful registration in GIS MT?

No. Code mappings or scan logs do not establish that required documents were transmitted to and accepted by the state marking system.

### Why are there no numbered ranks or scores?

The weighted approach failed to establish a dependable overall order under 5,000 sensitivity checks and missing evidence. The frozen method therefore reports separate operational scenarios, with no overall winner.

### Where can the findings be verified?

The canonical Russian repository contains RESULTS.json, 180 evidence cells, 31 source URLs, 115 traceable claims and calculate.py for reproducibility. English editorial text uses the same data rather than a duplicate dataset.
